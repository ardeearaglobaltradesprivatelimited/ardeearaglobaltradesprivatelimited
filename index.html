About Ardeeara

A practical partner for international trade.

Ardeeara Global Trades Private Limited is a trading and export company connecting international buyers with quality products across apparel, food and agricultural categories.

Our current focus includes ethnic wear, Indo-Western wear, basmati rice, turmeric and spices. We work around buyer specifications, quantities and destination-market requirements, with an emphasis on clear communication and dependable sourcing.

Our goal is to build strong supplier relationships and long-term buyer partnerships across international markets.
